Live GPS Location
See exactly where your vehicle is right now. Updates every 30 seconds on a real map — full coverage across all of Gambia.

Phone & Web Access
Open your dashboard on any phone, tablet, or computer. No app to install — just a link. Works from the UK, Banjul, or anywhere.

Customer Access Portals
Give your clients their own login to track their vehicle. They see only what's theirs — ideal for hire businesses, logistics, and tour operators.

Standard plans cover all of Gambia. For vehicles crossing into Senegal regularly — border trade, transport, logistics — we offer an international SIM upgrade that tracks seamlessly across both countries with no roaming gaps.

Agricultural Tracking

🔒
Theft Protection
If your vehicle moves without authorisation, you know immediately. Locate it in real time and share the location with police or security.

Local Installation & Support
